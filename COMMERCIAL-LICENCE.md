solicitor is to draft against. It is written plainly so that the intent is unambiguous

## What this repository is

CRUCIBLE is **dual-licensed**:

- **AGPL-3.0-or-later** — the licence in `LICENSE`, which governs this source for everyone.
- **A commercial grant** — an alternative licence, sold by The Dark Factory Ltd, which this
  document describes.

You do not need the commercial grant to use, study, modify or redistribute CRUCIBLE. The
AGPL already gives you all of that. You need it only for the one thing the AGPL does not
give you.

## What the commercial grant actually is

**The right to emit work under a licence of your choosing.**

The AGPL edition of CRUCIBLE emits AGPL-licensed work and refuses to emit anything else.
That refusal is how an honest AGPL build states its own licence. The commercial edition
does not carry that refusal.

★ **The refusal is a statement, not a lock.** This source is published. Anyone can build it
without the refusal, and nothing here pretends otherwise. What you buy is not a capability
the binary withholds from you — it is the **legal right** to emit under a licence that the
AGPL would not permit. Selling it as a technical lock would be selling something the code
cannot do, and we will not describe it that way.

## What is NOT sold

- Not the source. It is AGPL, and it stays AGPL.
- Not support, features, or priority. Those are separate if they are ever offered at all.
- Not an exception to the AGPL for anyone else's contributions — see `CLA.md` for why the
  contributor licence agreement exists and what it covers.
